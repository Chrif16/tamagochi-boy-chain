# Features
you can pet him, play with him, feed him, shake him, put him to sleep etc and he'll make some cute expressions and animations custom made by me.
he comes equiped with a gyroscope and a humidity and temperature sensor so he can help you out too
he has two buttons on his hands to control his expressions and to see his stats plus you can control the menu by tilting the gyroscope.

# How to build:

For screws and similar items, it is recommended to find the cheapest in your location. first 3d print all the parts in the cad folder except for the assembly, buy the pcb, flash the esp32, solder all the components on your pcb,charge your battery and put it in its spot, put your finished pcb inside the case and screw it shut and voila
